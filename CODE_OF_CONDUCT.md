# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Be clear. Assume good intent, but do not excuse harmful behavior.

We want this project to be a place where people can ask basic questions, give blunt technical feedback, and still treat each other well.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Doxxing
- Threats
- Repeated bad-faith disruption

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas, not people
- Keep feedback specific
- Be honest about uncertainty
- Leave room for other contributors to learn

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that crosses these lines.
